### 3.2 The AI chat on our website

On our website you can chat with an AI assistant. So you are not talking to a person. The assistant answers questions based on the information on our website.

| Data | Purpose | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| The messages you type | Answering your questions | Legitimate interest: informing visitors quickly (art. 6.1.f GDPR) |
| Name, email address, company, your question or need and the conversation, **only if you explicitly ask in the chat to be contacted** | Passing your details to our team so we can contact you | Consent (art. 6.1.a GDPR) and pre-contractual measures at your request (art. 6.1.b GDPR) |
| A hashed (not directly readable) form of your IP address, temporarily | Preventing abuse and overload of the chat | Legitimate interest (art. 6.1.f GDPR) |

How it works:

- To generate an answer, your messages are sent to the language model of Anthropic, which processes them as our processor. According to its terms, Anthropic stores inputs and outputs via the API temporarily (for a maximum of 30 days, or longer where needed to prevent abuse or required by law) and does not use them to train its models. The processing may take place outside the European Economic Area, with appropriate safeguards (see section 4.1).
- We do not store chat conversations on our own server. Only if you ask to be contacted do we receive the conversation by email at info@redfoxx.be (via Microsoft 365).
- If you book a call through the button in the chat, we pass the name and email address you gave in the chat to Calendly so the form is already filled in.
- Do not share sensitive data in the chat (health, financial data, passwords).
- Prefer a person? Email info@redfoxx.be or call +32 456 36 98 48.
- The chat contains a link to continue on WhatsApp. If you click it, WhatsApp's (Meta's) privacy policy applies to that conversation.

### 3.4 Business contacts we approach (prospecting)

This is our core activity. We approach companies, for ourselves or on behalf of a client (see 4.3), and look for the right contact person at that company.

**Which data:** name, job title, employer, business email address, business or mobile phone number, LinkedIn profile, and notes about our contacts (e.g. "call back in March", "not interested").

**Where do we get it (art. 14 GDPR):**

- public sources: the Crossroads Bank for Enterprises, annual accounts at the National Bank of Belgium (including the directors listed), company websites, press releases, LinkedIn;
- professional databases for business contact details: Apollo.io and Clay;
- contact lists that a client provides to us as part of an assignment.

**Purpose:** contacting companies with a business offer that is relevant to their role, and planning an introductory call.

**Legal basis:** legitimate interest (art. 6.1.f GDPR): business prospecting aimed at people in their professional role. We weigh our interest against your rights and freedoms. We limit ourselves to business data, only approach roles for which the offer is relevant, and stop immediately if you ask us to.

**Objection:** you can object to prospecting at any time, free of charge and without giving a reason (art. 21.2 GDPR). Reply to our message, tell us during the call, or email info@redfoxx.be. We then only keep your name and contact details on a suppression list so that we do not contact you again.

Our staff and regular partners who run campaigns have access to the data they need for that, and are bound by confidentiality.

### 4.3 When we work on behalf of a client

When we run a campaign or follow up inbound leads for a client, that client is the data controller and we are the processor. We then process the data only according to the client's instructions and the agreement with that client. We share booked appointments, notes and recordings with that client. If you want to exercise your rights for such a campaign, you can contact us or the client. We will pass your request on.

## 5. How long do we keep data?

| Data | Retention period |
|---|---|
| Server logs of the website | According to the settings of our hosting provider |
| Google Analytics | According to the setting in Google Analytics (maximum 14 months) |
| Chat messages at Anthropic | Maximum 30 days (according to Anthropic's terms) |

| Data processed on behalf of a client | According to the agreement with the client; by default returned or deleted at the end of the assignment |
| Clients and suppliers | Duration of the cooperation; invoices and accounting records 7 years (statutory retention period) |
| Job applicants | Up to 6 months after the end of the procedure; with your consent up to 2 years for future vacancies |

## 6. How do we protect your data?

We take appropriate technical and organisational measures, including: access only for those who need it, strong passwords and two-step verification where possible, encrypted connections (https), and no API keys in public code. The chat rejects requests from other websites and limits the number of messages per visitor.

## 7. Your rights

You have the right to:

- access your data;
- have incorrect data corrected;
- erasure;
- restriction of processing;
- data portability;
- object, in particular to prospecting (we always respect this, see 3.4);
- withdraw your consent, without affecting earlier processing.

Email info@redfoxx.be. We reply within one month. We may ask you to confirm your identity.

Not satisfied? You can lodge a complaint with the Belgian Data Protection Authority, Drukpersstraat 35, 1000 Brussels, contact@apd-gba.be, www.dataprotectionauthority.be.

## 8. Automated decision-making

We do not take decisions about people that are based solely on automated processing and that have legal effects (art. 22 GDPR). We do use AI tools to find business data, summarise conversations and prepare texts. A person decides whom we contact.
